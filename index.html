How it works

Click a square to place a note. Drag sideways from a note to make it longer. Drag across empty squares to paint. Right-click (or drag with the Erase tool) to rub notes out.

The playhead stays put when you pause. Drag it along the numbered bar at the top of the grid to move to any point in the song — playback picks up from wherever you drop it.

Recording

Press Record then Play and sing, hum, or play a MIDI keyboard — notes land on the selected track, snapped to the grid and to your scale. You can also just tap the letter keys in time.

Sharps & flats

By default the grid shows only the notes of your scale, so everything you draw sounds right. Turn on Extra notes in Settings to get the in-between semitones as recessed rows — the black keys — for accidentals and key changes. You can also switch the row names between sharps (C♯) and flats (D♭).

Your library

Open Share to see every song you have saved, each with a little picture of its notes. Songs live in this browser — no account, nothing uploaded — so clearing your browsing data would erase them. Export a .json file or copy a link for anything you want to keep or move to another device.

Patterns & song

Each of the eight patterns is a full grid of its own. Build a few, then chain them in the Song strip and switch the transport from Loop to Song to hear the arrangement end to end.
